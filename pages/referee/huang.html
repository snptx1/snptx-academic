Your letter is most valuable on standing and trajectory across the degree, the view only the program can offer. Please speak, from where you sit, to how the ALM has actually gone since the foundations course, and to the arc from that first data-engineering groundwork to a single-author biomedical discovery system that now anchors the applicant's capstone. This is an institutional and trajectory letter, distinct from the course-and-project letters, and that is exactly its value for the programs that accept a fourth or an institutional reference.

  • Standing in the program: how the applicant sits within the ALM and against the degree's expectations, from the vantage of the program director.
  • Trajectory: the arc from CSCI E-101 foundations, the data-engineering groundwork of moving, transforming, and managing data, through advanced deep learning, data mining, NLP, dynamic modeling, and epidemiology.
  • The through line: a single-author autonomous biomedical discovery system that now anchors the ALM Data Science capstone in Spring 2027.
  • Independent maturation: sustained, self-directed growth across the degree without a conventional lab affiliation.
  • Fit of the letter: an institutional and standing reference, complementary to the three course-and-project letters, for programs that accept a fourth or institutional letter.

No review is required, and there is no single graded artifact to revisit; this letter is pitched at standing and trajectory rather than at one project. The applicant's record across the degree, linked below, is the substance.
