What to emphasize

The letter is most valuable on standing and trajectory, the view only the program can offer. From where you sit, how has the degree actually gone since the foundations course? The arc runs from that first data-engineering groundwork to a single-author biomedical discovery system that now anchors the capstone, and no single-course letter can trace it. That makes this an institutional letter by design, and that is its value wherever a program welcomes a fourth or institutional reference.

  • Standing in the program: where I sit within the ALM and against the degree's expectations, from the vantage of the program director.
  • Trajectory: from CSCI E-101 foundations, the data-engineering groundwork of moving, transforming, and managing data, through advanced deep learning, data mining, NLP, dynamic modeling, and epidemiology.
  • The through line: a single-author autonomous biomedical discovery system that now anchors the ALM Data Science capstone in Spring 2027.
  • Independent maturation: sustained, self-directed growth across the degree without a conventional lab behind it.
  • Fit of the letter: an institutional and standing reference that complements the three course-and-project letters wherever a program accepts a fourth.

There is no single graded artifact to revisit and nothing to review; this letter is pitched at trajectory rather than at one project. The record across the degree, linked below, is the substance.

The statements, in brief

It walks through SNPTX, the engine I built as a single author; a train-test leak I caught and publicly corrected; the three doctoral-scale research questions that follow; and the faculty who would hold the work to that standard.
